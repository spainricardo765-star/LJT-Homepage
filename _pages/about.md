I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in the HKUST NLP Group at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024 - Present). My PhD supervisor is Professor Junxian He, who also supervised me during my undergraduate studies. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024, where I obtained my B.Eng. degree (2020 - 2024).

## Research Interests
My research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML), with specific interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
